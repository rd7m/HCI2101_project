Course Description

This course provides an in-depth introduction to 3D modelling and animation, equipping students with the technical and creative skills necessary to design and animate 3D assets. Students will explore the principles of 3D design, texturing, rigging, animation, and rendering, focusing on creating assets for games, simulations, and other interactive media. Using industry-standard tools such as Autodesk Maya, Blender, and Unreal Engine, students will gain hands-on experience in creating 3D models and animations while aligning their work with Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) principles for usability and engagement.

Course Main Objectives

  • Master the principles of 3D modeling, texturing, rigging, animation, and rendering to create high-quality 3D assets.
  • Develop hands-on expertise in tools like Autodesk Maya, Blender, and Unreal Engine for designing and animating 3D models.
  • Design 3D assets tailored for games, simulations, and other interactive applications, focusing on functionality and engagement.
  • Align 3D models and animations with HCI principles to enhance usability, accessibility, and player engagement in digital environments.

Course Content

  1. Introduction to 3D Modelling and Animation and environment setup — 4 hrs
  2. Modelling fundamentals (topology and edge flow) — 4 hrs
  3. Subdivision modelling and sculpting — 4 hrs
  4. Texturing and UV mapping — 4 hrs
  5. Basics of rigging: bones, joints, and skinning — 4 hrs
  6. Lighting and rendering — 4 hrs
  7. Procedural animation techniques — 4 hrs
  8. Integration with game engines — 4 hrs
  9. Principles of environmental design for games and simulations — 4 hrs
  10. Facial animation and lip-syncing — 4 hrs
  11. Optimization techniques for 3D assets — 4 hrs
  12. Keyframes and interpolation — 4 hrs
  13. Autodesk Maya hands-on — 4 hrs
  14. Blender and Unity for 3D assets — 4 hrs
